# Free plugins for techno and adjacent hard genres

Verified free, macOS, VST3/AU, Apple Silicon native, organised by the FL Studio
stock plugin each one substitutes for. Everything here was checked against the
developer's own site or repo. Anything unverified is flagged.

## Start here — ten plugins, all native, none needing an account

**Surge XT** (synth) · **ChowKick** (kick body) · **BYOD** (distortion chains) ·
**Free Clip 2** (clipping) · **Xfer OTT** (density) · **LoudMax** (limiter) ·
**TDR Nova** (dynamic EQ) · **ValhallaSupermassive** (reverb) · **Pumpit**
(ducking) · **TX16Wx** (break chopping)

Add **Vital** if a free account is acceptable — for wavetable work it's worth it.

## Kick design — no FL stock equivalent, and the weakest free category

[**ChowKick**](https://chowdsp.com/products.html) (VST3/AU/CLAP, BSD, open
source) is the only free, verified Apple-Silicon-native dedicated kick synth.
Models drum-machine circuitry: trigger pulse into a tuned resonant filter.

There is no free Kick 2 or RAW-Kick equivalent. The realistic free workflow:

1. **Surge XT or Vital** as the source (sine + pitch envelope, click layer), or
   **ChowKick** for the body
2. a distortion/clipper chain (below)
3. resample, then layer in a drum sampler

That layer-and-resample approach is what most hardcore and frenchcore producers
do anyway, kick synth or not.

## Limiting — replaces Fruity Limiter

[**LoudMax**](https://loudmax.blogspot.com/) — transparent brickwall that stays
clean under heavy gain reduction and doesn't smear kick transients. Correct
chain for these genres is clipper → LoudMax, not limiter alone.

> TDR Limiter 6 GE is **no longer free** (€60, demo blocks recall) despite what
> most listicles say.

## EQ — replaces Parametric EQ 2

[**TDR Nova**](https://www.tokyodawn.net/tdr-nova/) is arguably the most useful
free plugin on this list: a dynamic EQ, so you can duck one frequency of the
bass against the kick instead of static-notching the mix to death. Also
[TDR SlickEQ](https://www.tokyodawn.net/tdr-vos-slickeq/) for broad tonal
shaping and [Voxengo Marvel GEQ](https://www.voxengo.com/product/marvelgeq/).

## Sidechain ducking — replaces Peak Controller + Limiter

[**Pumpit**](https://www.remiblaze.com/plugins/pumpit/) is the standout find:
three trigger modes (tempo-synced / transient detection / MIDI), 10 curves plus
a 32-point editor, 5 ms lookahead with PDC, and **multiband ducking** so a
frenchcore kick can duck the bass without gutting hats and screeches. A genuine
free Kickstart replacement, no account.

[**TAL-Filter-2**](https://tal-software.com/products/tal-filter) draws one
host-synced envelope across volume, panning *and* filter at once — an LFOTool
substitute for free.

> Honest note: Fruity Peak Controller is more flexible (it drives any
> parameter). But at 180–250 BPM, envelope-follower ducking smears when kicks
> are 250 ms apart; a drawn envelope gives an identical pump every bar.

## Do not bother — dead, paid, or Intel-only

These circulate constantly in "best free plugins" lists and are wrong:

- **CamelCrusher** — Camel Audio was acquired by Apple in 2015, downloads ended.
  `camelcrusher.com` is an unaffiliated mirror; treat binaries as untrusted.
- **DistoCore Bazz::Murda** — `distocore.com` is a parked domain for sale.
- **NoizeClick** — now €25, and its own page says it's incompatible with
  Catalina and Big Sur.
- **Kilohearts Snap Heap** — now $29.
- **TDR Limiter 6 GE** — now €60.
- **TAL-Dub-3** — 32-bit only, needs macOS < 10.10.
- **Limiter No6** — AU/VST2 only, no longer actively supported.
- **OrilRiver** — development suspended, AU validation failures reported.
- **Sitala** — the free 1.0 is Intel-only; Apple Silicon needs the paid v2.1.
- **Grace** — Windows-only, no macOS build exists.
- **sfizz** — repos archived June 2026, unmaintained. Use sforzando.
- **"Fast Ducker"** — no such product exists from any developer.
- **MiniSpillage** — AU-only, Apple Silicon only in the *paid* DrumSpillage.
- **Dragonfly Reverb**, **Ignite Amps**, **CloudSeed**, **Speedrum Lite** —
  unverified on Apple Silicon; test before relying on them.
